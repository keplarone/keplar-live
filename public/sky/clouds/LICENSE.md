# Sky cloud cutouts

Four cumulus sprites, `cumulus-1.webp` through `cumulus-4.webp`.

Source: “Clouds with Transparency” by WickedInsignia, rendered in Blender.
https://opengameart.org/content/clouds-with-transparency

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The author released the renders into the public domain for commercial and non-commercial use. Attribution is not required. These files were cropped, given a white-to-grey shade, feathered on the alpha so the rims stay soft, resized, and re-encoded as WebP for keplar.one. The noisier source renders were left out.
