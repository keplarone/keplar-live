A small business usually needs a clear site more than a clever one: what you do, where you are, how to reach you and one thing a visitor should do next. Create is Keplar's builder for that first version.

## What you provide

- What the business does, in a few plain sentences.
- Who your customers are.
- The one action you want visitors to take, such as calling, booking or buying.
- Facts that must be right: hours, address, services and prices.
- Photos if you have them.

You can also import a product from a page or photos to start a simple shop. Details are in [Build a site](/docs/create/build-a-site) and [Import a product](/docs/create/import-a-product).

## What happens

Create asks a few discovery questions, shows an estimate before it spends credits, and then generates the site in one of eight style directions. You can review and edit it, change the style, and publish to a free Keplar address. Plus and above can connect a custom domain: one on Plus, three on Pro and ten on Business.

## Checks before you publish

1. **Facts.** Hours, address, phone, prices, claims. Generated text can invent details; replace anything you did not provide.
2. **Photos.** Use pictures you own or have a license for.
3. **Legal basics.** Add the policies your location and business require.
4. **The one action.** Does the page make it obvious?
5. **Mobile.** Open the preview on a phone.
6. **Contact form.** Submit a test and confirm you receive it.

## Forms and leads

The contact form on a published site creates a lead in your Business tools, with spam protection built in. On Plus and above, an agent can draft a reply for your approval. See [Business tools](/docs/connectors-agents/business-tools).

## Selling

A shop's Buy button can go to a payment link you provide. Seller payments through Stripe Connect only work when that account is connected; until then, the store says it is not accepting payments and no card is collected.

## Safety and responsibility

Keplar sanitizes site content, does not run user-written scripts, and gives every site an abuse-report link. You are responsible for what you publish. See [Published sites and safety](/docs/privacy-security/published-sites-safety).

## What Create is not

It is not a guarantee of search rankings or a full e-commerce platform with every integration. It is a quick way to a clear first version. Limits per plan are in [Create limits by plan](/docs/create/create-limits-by-plan).
