Search with Keplar One works like a lens over the page you are on. Instead of copying text into a chat, you drag over what you are curious about: a paragraph, a chart, a product photo, a diagram. Keplar reads the words in that area and looks at a picture of it, asks several models, and answers in a panel beside the page.

## Open it

- Right-click anywhere on a page and choose **Search with Keplar One**.
- Or click the Keplar One toolbar button and press **Search with Keplar One**.

The first time, a card explains what a search sends and asks you to continue. Nothing is sent before you agree, and the card only appears once.

## Steps

1. The page dims and a pill at the top reads "Select any text or image to search with Keplar One".
2. Drag a box over the area. It lights up with a moving border, and the search starts by itself.
3. A side panel opens with the answer: how many models agreed, the answer itself, and where the models differed if they did.

Next to the area you get **Copy text** (the words inside it), **Translate** (into your browser's language) and **Copy as image**. In the panel, quick prompts such as **Explain this** or **Is this accurate?** run a new search on the same area.

## Follow up

Type into **Ask a follow-up** at the bottom of the panel. Keplar keeps the earlier questions and answers of that search, so "which side has better evidence?" makes sense. Dragging a new area starts a fresh search.

## Close it

Press Escape or the close button. Scrolling the page is paused while the overlay is open.

## Good to know

- Pictures are answered by models that can read images, so a search with a picture may use a few more credits than a text question.
- It covers the browser tab you opened it on, not other windows or the rest of your screen.
- Models can agree and still be wrong. For anything that matters, check the original source.
