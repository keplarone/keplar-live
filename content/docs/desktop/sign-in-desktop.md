The desktop app does not ask for your password. It hands sign-in to your browser, where you use whichever method you normally use.

## Steps

1. Open the desktop app and choose Sign in.
2. Your browser opens a Keplar page to connect the app. Sign in there if you are not already.
3. Confirm that you want to connect the desktop app.
4. Return to the app. It is now signed in to the same account as the browser.

## Why a browser handoff

Sign-in methods such as Google and Microsoft run best in a browser, and a browser keeps your password and email links out of the app. The handoff uses a short-lived code that is exchanged once.

## Switching accounts

Use the account menu in the app to add or switch accounts, as in the browser. Signing out of the desktop app does not sign you out of the browser.

## Same plan, same data

Because the app is a client for the cloud service, plans, credits, chats, Study sets and sites are the same as on the web.

## If the handoff fails

See [Desktop troubleshooting](/docs/desktop/desktop-troubleshooting): the usual causes are a blocked pop-up or protocol handler, an expired code, or a browser profile that is signed in to a different account than you expected.
