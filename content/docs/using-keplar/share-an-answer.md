Nothing is shared unless you create a link. When you do, Keplar shows exactly what will be in it first.

## What a link shows

One answer: the written answer, how many models agreed, which models were consulted with their stance, and the disagreements. The question is included only if you leave it ticked.

## What it never includes

Your account id, name or email, your history, or your memory. A page view only increments a counter; it records no IP address and no user agent.

## Properties of a shared page

- Text is rendered as plain text and links in the answer are not clickable.
- The page is marked noindex, nofollow and noarchive, so search engines are asked not to list it.
- Links expire after 90 days.
- You can revoke a link at any time, and it then returns a real "not found" page immediately.

## Optional: make it a public page

A private link is never listed in search engines. If you want an answer to be found, press **Make public** after creating the link. This is a second, separate choice.

- Needs a verified email address, a question included (it becomes the page title) and an answer of reasonable length.
- You tick a box confirming the page will be public and search engines may index it.
- The page gets its own address under keplar.one/answers, a preview image, and is listed on the Shared answers page. It never includes your name, email, history or memory.
- Keplar checks for email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, keys and similar. If it finds one it refuses to publish and says what it found. This check is a safety net, not a guarantee: re-read the page yourself, especially names and private details in free text.
- You can have up to 10 public pages at a time. A public page stays up until you make it private, revoke the link or delete your account. It does not expire after 90 days.
- Making it private or revoking the link returns a "not found" page on keplar.one at once. Search engines may keep a cached copy for a while.
- Keplar can take down a public page that is reported or that breaks the Terms.

## Limits

You must be signed in. You can have at most 25 live links and create at most 10 per hour. These limits exist to prevent abuse.

## Steps

1. Open an answer and choose Share.
2. Review what will be included. Untick the question if it is private.
3. Create the link and copy it.
4. Optional: choose Make public to publish it for search engines.
5. To stop sharing, open your list of links and revoke it, or choose Make private.

## Reporting a shared page

If you see a shared page that breaks the rules, report it through the [feedback form](/feedback) with the problem kind; the team can remove a link.

## Why share an answer rather than a screenshot

The shared page keeps the agreement counts and the disagreements, which a screenshot of the final text loses. That makes it easier for the person you send it to to see how firm the answer is.
