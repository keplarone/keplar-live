Plans differ in three things: which models can answer, how much you can do (credits and rolling limits), and which features are unlocked. They do not change which kind of question gets how many models; that depends on the question.

## In one paragraph each

**Free.** Real Keplar on free open models. Up to 50 questions a day under fair use, image and video questions on free vision models, Study mode with smaller limits, saved chats and optional memory when signed in. Can be slower when free models are busy, and free model hosts may log prompts. No Deep Research, no web lookups, no Create beyond the basics.

**Keplar Lite.** The step up: premium models for everyday questions at a lower price. Monthly credits with a 5-hour and a weekly limit, coding help and file analysis, Study mode on stronger models, and a small personal site on a free keplar.site address. Image generation, Slides, Deep Research, video, agents and custom domains start at Plus.

**Keplar Plus.** For daily use. A larger credit allowance, Deep Research, image generation, the slideshow creator with PowerPoint and PDF export, personal sites and apps, custom domains, connected apps and a few agents.

**Keplar Pro.** For heavy use and creation: more than twice Plus's monthly credits, video generation, the largest Study and Slides limits, more agents and higher priority.

**Business.** Custom. Commerce, CRM, inbox, a dashboard and agents with hard spend caps and human approval gates, with audit logs and an emergency stop. Contact Keplar through the Inquire form.

## A sensible way to choose

1. Start on Free and ask the questions you actually have.
2. If you hit the daily limit or want stronger models, try Lite.
3. If you want Deep Research, images, slides, sites with your own domain or connected apps, you need Plus.
4. Move to Pro when you keep reaching Plus's limits or need video.

You can change plans later; see [Upgrade, downgrade and cancel](/docs/billing-plans/upgrade-downgrade-cancel).
