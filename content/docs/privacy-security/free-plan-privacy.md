Free is funded by free open models served by third-party hosts. The cost is not money; it is that those hosts set their own data rules.

## The difference

| | Free | Plus and Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Models | Free open models only | Premium and open models |
| Host logging | Hosts **may log** prompts | OpenRouter requests restricted to endpoints that say they do not collect or train on prompts |
| Training by hosts | Some hosts **may use prompts** to improve models | Direct OpenAI and NVIDIA calls are handled under their API terms |
| Keplar training | Does not train on your data | Does not train on your data |

## What this means in practice

- A Free question may be seen by a host Keplar does not control.
- You cannot ask a host to forget it through Keplar.
- Deleting a chat removes it from Keplar. It cannot remove data a provider keeps under its own terms.

## What to keep out of Free

Passwords, keys, account numbers, health or legal details about you or others, unpublished work you do not want a third party to see, and anything you promised to keep confidential. If it is sensitive, do not ask it on Free.

## Why Keplar is open about it

Because it is a trade-off, not a flaw. The Free screen and pricing tiers state it, and so does the Privacy Policy. Keplar Free never routes you to a paid model without telling you.

## If you need more

A paid plan sends requests only to endpoints that state they do not collect or train on prompts, though providers may retain requests for a limited time. Business customers who need contractual terms can use the Inquire form.
