A site you publish with Create is served from a Keplar address or your own domain. Keplar designs around the fact that anyone can build a site, including people who should not.

## What the platform enforces

- **Sanitized content.** Site content is cleaned on the server against an allowlist before it is stored or served.
- **No user scripts.** Sites do not run scripts you write. Interactivity (like a contact form) comes from Keplar-controlled components.
- **Isolation.** Published pages are served with restrictive security headers, including a sandboxing content security policy.
- **Forms.** Contact forms use a honeypot and rate limits.
- **Reports.** Every published site carries a way to report abuse, and reports are stored for review.
- **Limits.** Plans cap projects, domains and generation so an account cannot create unlimited sites.

## What you are responsible for

You own what you publish and must have the right to use its text, images and products. Do not publish content that is illegal, deceptive, infringing, or that targets or harms people. Photos you upload should be yours or licensed. Keplar can unpublish a site that breaks the [Terms](/terms).

## Payments

A shop only takes payments when you connect a payment account. Otherwise the Buy button does not collect cards and the page says so.

## Search engines

Published sites are indexable only if you choose. Keplar's own shared answer links are noindex.

## Reporting a site

Use the report link on the site, or write to [team@keplar.one](mailto:team@keplar.one). Reports of the most serious kinds are handled with priority.
