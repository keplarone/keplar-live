## Sharing

- A share link lives for 90 days.
- Up to 25 live links per account.
- Up to 10 new links per hour.
- Shared pages are marked noindex.

## Time

- A whole answer has a time budget of about 60 seconds. Panels may use at most about 55 percent of it, and slow models are not waited for beyond a short grace once enough have answered.

## Agents (default caps)

1,500 credits a month, 24 runs a day, 20 sends a day and $3 a month per agent. See [Agent guardrails](/docs/connectors-agents/agent-guardrails).

## Where limits are enforced

On the server, so refreshing or opening another device does not reset them. Details in [Rolling usage limits](/docs/billing-plans/usage-windows).
