## Notes

- **Lite billing.** Lite is shown as {{price:lite}} a month; the exact amount is shown at checkout, which is billed through Whop, the payment processor.
- **Free.** Runs on free open models only and never falls back to a paid model. It can be slower when free models are busy, and free hosts may log prompts. See [Free plan limits](/docs/billing-plans/free-plan-limits).
- **Business.** Custom contract with commerce, CRM, inbox, a dashboard and agents with hard caps and approval gates. Contact Keplar through the Inquire form.
- **What a credit is.** See [Credits explained](/docs/billing-plans/credits-explained).

## What does not change by plan

How many models a question gets depends on the question, not your plan. The honesty guards (missing models never counted as dissent, no invented disagreements, exact checks) apply on every plan. The Free plan simply caps panels at three models.
