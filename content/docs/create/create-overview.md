Create is where Keplar makes things instead of answering questions: a personal site, product pages, images, and (on higher plans) video. You can reach it from the composer's Create option or from the Projects page.

## What is in Create

| Area | What it does | Plans |
|---|---|---|
| Sites | Describe a site; Keplar asks a few questions, shows an editable brief, builds a multi-page site with a live preview, and keeps versions you can undo | Free to Business, with different project counts |
| Product import | Start from a product link or photo instead of a description | Same as Sites, with higher daily limits on higher plans |
| Images | Generate an image from a prompt, keep a version history, insert it into a site | Plus, Pro and Business |
| Video (Beta) | Generate short clips; on Pro and Business, compare models side by side | Pro and Business |
| Domains | Serve a published site from your own domain | Plus, Pro and Business |

Project counts: Free 1, Lite 2, Plus 5, Pro 20, Business 100. Check the Create screen for your plan's current limits, since limits can change.

## How a build works

1. You answer 3 to 6 short questions, or press "Just build it" to skip them.
2. Keplar shows an editable **brief**: name, goal, audience, tone, pages, real content, style direction and motion, with a credit estimate.
3. You approve, and the model builds from the brief.
4. You see a live preview, edit by chatting ("make it dark", "add pricing"), and use version history to undo.
5. You publish to a free address, and optionally add a domain.

## Rules that apply to everything

- **No invented proof.** The generator is told not to write fake testimonials, statistics or claims. Where it has no real facts it writes a marked placeholder you must fill in.
- **No user scripts.** Published sites run no script of yours. See [Published site safety](/docs/privacy-security/published-sites-safety).
- **Credits are shown before you start.** You see an estimate; failed or refused jobs are not charged.
- **Moderation.** Prompts and outputs are checked. Sexual content, minors, deepfakes, graphic violence and real-person likeness are refused for images, and sites that collect cards, IDs, passwords or seed phrases are blocked from publishing.

## Where Create stops

Create makes static sites. A shop's Buy button links to a payment link you provide; sites have no built-in checkout or cart. Dynamic features like logins are not available in generated sites. Contact forms are supported and their messages land in your Business inbox.
