Limits exist to keep the service affordable and safe. These numbers were read from the product on 18 October 2026. They can change; the Create screen and the [pricing page](/pricing) always show the current ones.

Pictures and video are not Create tabs. A picture is made in chat with Create image, on Plus, Pro and Business. A clip is made by asking in chat, on Pro (up to 8 seconds) and Business (up to 10 seconds). Free, Lite and Plus cannot start a clip.

## Sites and domains

| | Free | Lite | Plus | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Projects | 1 | 2 | 5 | 20 | 100 |
| Free Keplar address | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Custom domains | 0 | 0 | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| Pictures in chat | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Video in chat (Beta) | No | No | No | Yes, up to 8 seconds | Yes, up to 10 seconds |
| Commerce tools | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Agents | 0 | 0 | 3 | 10 | 50 |

## Product import, per day

| | Free | Lite | Plus | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Page imports | 5 | 15 | 40 | 100 | 200 |
| Photo readings | 5 | 15 | 40 | 100 | 200 |
| Photos on one site | 3 | 6 | 10 | 16 | 24 |
| Images imported | 12 | 30 | 80 | 200 | 400 |

Stored product images also have a count and size cap per plan.

## Connected apps

Free has none. Plus allows 3 connections, Pro 10, Business 25, each with daily and per-minute call limits. See [Connected apps overview](/docs/connectors-agents/connected-apps-overview).

## What happens at a limit

You see a message that names the limit and the next plan, and the action does not run. Nothing is charged for an action that did not run. Limits that reset (daily, 5-hour, weekly) show when they reset. See [When you reach a limit](/docs/billing-plans/when-you-reach-a-limit).

## Downgrading

If you move to a lower plan, content stays in your account but features above your new allowance stop working: domains over the new allowance stop serving, and Create actions your plan no longer includes are locked until you upgrade again.

## Credits, separately

Everything in Create that calls a model uses credits as well. See [Credits explained](/docs/billing-plans/credits-explained).
