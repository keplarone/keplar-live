Video generation is in Beta. It is available on Pro and Business. Free, Lite and Plus cannot start a clip. Video is not part of Create: ask in an ordinary chat, with no mode selected.

## How it works

1. Ask for a clip in chat, for example "make a video of a fox in snow". A question about video ("how do I edit a video") is answered as a normal message.
2. If you did not already say the shape or the length, Keplar shows a card in the thread. Shape is **16:9** (landscape) or **9:16** (vertical). Length is only the durations the model and your plan both allow. Pro can go up to 8 seconds and Business up to 10. Press **Create video**. Enter confirms. Arrow keys move the highlighted choice.
3. Words in the message fill those choices in and skip the question. "Vertical", "portrait", "TikTok", "Reels" or "Shorts" means 9:16. "Landscape", "widescreen" or "YouTube" means 16:9. "16:9" and "9:16" are read as written. A length such as "10 seconds" or "8s" is used when that length is allowed. If both the shape and the length are already known and allowed, the clip starts without the card.
4. The last shape and length you confirmed are the highlighted defaults next time. They do not skip a question your message left open.
5. A large job shows the credit cost and waits for a second confirmation. Credits are taken only when the clip is delivered. Failed or canceled clips cost nothing.
6. The clip plays in the thread, with a download. Keplar checks the file's dimensions and length against what you asked for and says so if they differ.

With Create mode on, a video request is answered as a normal chat message. No clip is made.

## Limits you may hit

- **Clip length.** The card only offers lengths the current text-to-video model documents, cut by the plan maximum: 8 seconds on Pro, 10 on Business.
- **Windows.** The 5-hour and weekly [usage windows](/docs/billing-plans/usage-windows) still apply.
- **Plan.** Free, Lite and Plus see a short note and a link to plans. Nothing is submitted.

## Storage and playback

Clips are stored for you and can be downloaded from the thread. Because this feature is in Beta, check that a download plays where you will use it before building a project around it.

## Safety

The same moderation as images applies, including the rule against real-person likeness and deepfakes.

## Honest limit

Video models vary a lot in quality from prompt to prompt. Keplar shows the cost and the options but does not promise a particular result.
