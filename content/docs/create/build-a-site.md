## Steps

1. Open Create and describe what you want, in a sentence or two: "A one-page site for my dog-walking business in Austin."
2. Answer the discovery questions (3 to 6 tappable chips with free text). Each is skippable, and "Just build it" skips them all. Discovery itself makes no model call and costs nothing.
3. Review the brief. Edit anything: name, goal, audience, tone, pages, real content, style direction, motion.
4. Check the credit estimate and press **Approve and build**.
5. Watch the build stream in, then check the live preview on desktop and mobile widths.
6. Refine by chatting: "Make the headline shorter", "Add a pricing section", "Use a darker palette".
7. Open version history to compare or undo any change.
8. [Publish](/docs/create/publish-your-site) when you are happy.

## Give it real content

The generator writes from your brief. The more real facts you provide (name, services, prices, hours, a contact address), the less placeholder text you will see. Fields that Keplar had no facts for are marked as to-do placeholders, which a quality check flags so you do not publish them by accident.

## Style and effects

Eight style directions provide font pairings and palettes checked for readable contrast in light and dark. Changing the style re-applies the design without calling a model and costs no credits. Optional motion effects (reveals, parallax, and a small set of particle or contour scenes) can be turned on per site and set to an intensity; they respect reduced-motion settings and fall back to still images on weak devices or Save-Data connections. They are off by default unless your brief asks for motion.

## Quality pass

After a build, an automated pass looks for filler placeholder text, emoji used as icons, clichéd copy, invented proof, missing alt text and labels, heading problems, fixed widths that overflow, and low contrast. Mechanical problems are repaired automatically; a single model repair pass handles the rest (it is included at the high end of the estimate); and a final pass removes unverified quotes.

## Projects page

All your sites are listed with a thumbnail, status (Live, Draft or Needs update), versions and last change. You can rename, duplicate, archive and restore (not while published), and open a drawer with the live URL, the ZIP, leads CSV and activity.

## Export

Download a ZIP of the current version at any time. The Code tab shows each file. Your site is yours to take elsewhere.

## Limits

Project counts and page counts depend on your plan, and Lite builds are small. Builds can take a few minutes; a build stops at the time limit rather than running forever. See [Create limits by plan](/docs/create/create-limits-by-plan).
