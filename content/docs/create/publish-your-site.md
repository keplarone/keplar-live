## Steps

1. Verify your email (required to publish).
2. In Create, open your site and go to the Publish tab.
3. Check the SEO fields: site title, description, link-preview image and per-page overrides.
4. Decide whether search engines may index the site. Indexing is **off** unless you turn it on.
5. Press Publish. A scan runs first.
6. Share the live address. Optionally [add your own domain](/docs/create/custom-domains) on Plus and above.

## The free address

Published sites are served on a Keplar address. Reserved words and look-alike names are refused. Free accounts can publish one site; project counts rise with the plan.

## The pre-publish scan

The scan blocks sites that collect card numbers, IDs, seed phrases or passwords, look-alike sign-in pages for known brands, links to malware files, URL shorteners, raw-IP links and scam wording. Borderline results publish and are queued for a human look.

## SEO for your site

Titles, descriptions and Open Graph tags are added to the page head with a canonical link. A `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are generated per site. If you turn indexing on, your site can appear in search; if you leave it off, it carries a noindex instruction.

## Forms and leads

Contact forms post to Keplar, with a honeypot field and per-IP and per-site rate limits. Messages are stored and shown in the Leads tab, can be exported as CSV (safe against spreadsheet formula injection), and flow into the Business inbox and CRM if you use those tools. A shop's Buy button goes to the link you configured.

## Abuse reports

Every published page has a Report abuse link. Reports are stored; reports about child sexual abuse material or non-consensual intimate imagery take the site down immediately and alert the owner.

## Updating a live site

Edit in Create, check the preview, and publish again. Version history lets you go back. Unpublishing returns the address to "not found".
