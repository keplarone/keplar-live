Up to 4 booms overlap (one uniform slot each); a fast second click never cuts the first off (a 5th click reuses the boom furthest through its life). The camera nudge is a damped spring that changes velocity, never position, and the idle camera drift now fades in/out instead of switching (a small existing jump when a scene had no camera pose).
