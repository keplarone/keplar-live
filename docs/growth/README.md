## One-paragraph summary
Chosen segment: independent developers and technical solo founders. Job: pressure-test a technical or product decision with a multi-model second opinion that shows where the models split. Positioning: "Get a second opinion before you commit to the technical decision." Two channels: (A) founder-led demos and rule-abiding developer communities, (B) original, dated decision guides. Largest obstacle: no reachable, measured audience (plus a rarely-appearing disagreement view and a Free-capacity ceiling). Cost per successful task ~$0.0085; revenue targets and the traffic they need are in MODEL.md.
