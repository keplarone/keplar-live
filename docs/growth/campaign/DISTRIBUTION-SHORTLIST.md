# Shortlist: 15 distribution opportunities (all free; verified 3 Oct 2026)

How to read the **Verified** column: **Opened** = the rules/FAQ page was fetched in this work and the statement below is from it. **Search** = seen only in search results; confirm on the page before acting. **No contact details are invented**: the only public address found is `hello@console.dev` (printed on its own page). Everything else is a submission form, an account-based flow or a reply to a newsletter you subscribe to. Nothing was submitted, posted or sent.

| # | Opportunity | Verified reason it is relevant (what the page says) | Source | Verified | Fit / action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Show HN** | Show HN is for things people can try; "easy to try, ideally without signups"; no asking for upvotes; no marketing language. Keplar `/app` works without an account. | https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html | Opened | Best fit for Channel A. One post, one founder account, Saturday/Sunday not required. Lead with the honest Node-LTS failure. Do not post two Show HNs (see COORDINATION.md). |
| 2 | **Product Hunt** | Free launch; personal accounts only (no company account); do not ask for upvotes. | https://www.producthunt.com/launch | Opened (by the SEO workstream, same day) | Later, after the first 100 activated users (a one-day spike with no retention proof helps little). |
| 3 | **DEV Community** | Terms: content must be on-topic and substantial, not primarily promotion/backlinks; canonical URL supported. | https://dev.to/terms | Opened (SEO workstream) | Channel A/B cross-post of a guide with canonical to keplar.one. |
| 4 | **Hashnode** | Free blog; supports importing/setting a canonical URL. | https://hashnode.com | Opened | Cross-post of a guide with canonical. Optional. |
| 5 | **DevHunt** | Free listing through a queue, sign-in required, developer tools only; free link is `nofollow`; paid $19/$49 options exist (not used). | https://devhunt.org/faq | Opened | One-time listing. Keplar for developers fits "developer tool" only if framed as a decision tool; low effort. |
| 6 | **Uneed** | Free submissions wait in a queue of up to about 5 months and can be declined; "Fast-track" $14.99 and "skip" $29.99 exist (not used). | https://help.uneed.best/getting-started/what-happens-after-you-submit | Opened | Submit once; do not rely on it. |
| 7 | **SaaSHub** | Submission with approval process; the product must be on its own domain; verification by a domain email. | https://www.saashub.com/services/submit | Opened | One-time listing; needs a `@keplar.one` mailbox (owner access item). |
| 8 | **AlternativeTo** | Free: sign up, "Suggest new application"; the review backlog is "at least a few months"; a $5 priority review exists (not used); **declines "thin wrapper around someone else's model" apps and AI tool directories**. | https://alternativeto.net/faq/ | Opened | Risk of rejection: Keplar orchestrates multiple models and verifies, but it may be judged a wrapper. Submit only if founders accept a likely decline. |
| 9 | **Indie Hackers** (product page + groups) | The Products directory lists self-submitted indie products with self-reported revenue; groups allow founder-story posts (rules: no press-release posts, no cross-posting). | https://www.indiehackers.com/products | Opened (directory); posting rules Search | Create the product page honestly (no revenue to report: leave blank). Post a build-in-public story once with real numbers (e.g., the 13-run test). |
| 10 | **r/SideProject** | Rules page could not be fetched (403/timeout); search results say showing your work is allowed if you explain what it does and ask for specific feedback; no vote begging. | https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/ | Search | Read the live sidebar rules before posting; use a founder's real account with history; one post, reply to every comment. |
| 11 | **Changelog News** | Accepts submissions of own work but **not commercial products or how-tos**; an analysis essay can fit. | https://changelog.com/news/submit | Opened | Only the non-commercial essay "Three models agreed and were all out of date" (no product push) might fit. Optional. |
| 12 | **Console.dev** | Weekly developer-tools newsletter; selection criteria say tools should be beta/pre-1.0 labeled; contact `hello@console.dev` printed on the page; features 2-3 tools a week. | https://console.dev/selection-criteria | Opened | **Not eligible as it stands**; do not relabel the product as beta just to qualify. Listed so nobody wastes a message. |
| 13 | **Postgres Weekly** | Curated Postgres newsletter; issues end with "Got a link for us? Reply and tell us." (search-derived); the homepage shows 667 issues since 2013. | https://postgresweekly.com/ | Homepage Opened; reply flow Search | Only for the JSONB-vs-normalized guide, once published and genuinely useful to Postgres readers; reply from your own subscription. Not for a product pitch. |
| 14 | **Peerlist Launchpad** | Help center: profiles are personal (using an individual profile as a company profile or publishing articles for SEO/backlink farming gets suspended). The weekly Launchpad is for builders. | https://help.peerlist.io/ | Opened (help center: suspension rules); Launchpad rules Search | Optional, personal founder profile only; follow their rules. |
| 15 | **OpenRouter app attribution** | Apps that send `HTTP-Referer` and `X-Title` can appear in OpenRouter's app rankings/pages; Keplar already sends both. | https://openrouter.ai/docs/app-attribution | Opened | No submission needed. Check whether a Keplar app page exists after traffic; claim nothing about ranking. |

Not on the list on purpose: BetaList (paid per the SEO workstream's check), There's An AI For That ($49 paid review; SEO workstream), paid fast-lanes of Uneed/DevHunt/AlternativeTo, Lobsters (invite-only), any influencer or creator (none verified; see [OUTREACH-DRAFTS.md](OUTREACH-DRAFTS.md)).

## How each is tracked
Every link uses `utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=<post|listing|newsletter>&utm_campaign=<piece-id>` to `/for-developers` or a guide; the consented funnel records landing, activation and sign-up by source. Log date, URL and outcome in [../PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md). Judge with the rules in [../EXPERIMENTS.md](../EXPERIMENTS.md) (X1/X2).
