# Content and distribution calendar (relative weeks; milestones, not promised dates)

Everything is founder-posted; nothing is automated. Two channels only: A (demos + communities), B (decision guides). Capacity check before each post: Free usage under 70% of ~150 questions a day.

| Week | Founder A (about 3 h) | Founder B (about 3 h) | Autonomous (drafting, checks) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review docs; decide Vercel plan and Free-capacity approach; send research form to 10 known developers | Review positioning; record V1 (Node LTS) from the live product | Hand-offs (COORDINATION); verify analytics shows consenting visits; finalize guides 1-2 |
| 2 | Send research form to 10-20 more; read responses | Post Show HN (one post) + answer comments for 24 h | Re-run R-prompts on posting day, save streams |
| 3 | Publish guide 1 (JSONB) + DEV cross-post (canonical) | Record V2 (Rust) | Weekly review numbers |
| 4 | Indie Hackers product page + post (P8) | Publish guide 2 (JWT) | Update PROGRESS; check indexing |
| 5-6 | Guide 3, V3, DevHunt/Uneed/SaaSHub listings | Guide 4, V4 | Compare X1/X2 counters vs minimum evidence |
| 7-8 | Respond to research findings; fix top 2 friction points | Guide 5, V5, Changelog essay (optional) | Retention/feedback review |
| 9-12 | Guide 6 (Node LTS essay), tutorials T1/T2 | V6 share demo (after live check) | Decide: continue/stop each channel using EXPERIMENTS.md |

Post-launch cadence: 1 guide every 1-2 weeks, 1 clip a week, Friday review. Product Hunt only after the first 100 activated users.
