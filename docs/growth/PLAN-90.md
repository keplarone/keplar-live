# 90-day plan

Goals are goals, not forecasts. Stage gates are **milestones, not dates**: move on when the evidence exists. Paid acquisition stays a proposal until a budget, a loss limit and written authorization exist. Hours: assume each founder has about 3-4 hours a week for growth (an assumption; adjust to reality). Autonomous = work the executor/agents can do in the repo without a founder; Founder = needs a human identity, account, decision or money.

## Stage gates
1. **First 100 activated users** (activation per [ACTIVATION.md](ACTIVATION.md), from consenting traffic plus signed-in accounts). Before this, no paid tests, no Product Hunt, no referral rewards.
2. **First 10 paying customers** (Whop-confirmed). Then: interview/survey them, review refunds and cancel reasons, check Lite vs Plus fit.
3. **First 100 retained paying customers** (still paying after 2 billing cycles). Then expand what's supported; consider annual plans and a case study with permission.

## Days 1-14: verify, choose, measure, prepare
- Autonomous (done or in this package): baseline, segment, positioning, model, measurement and dashboard, activation changes, sharing, feedback form, campaign assets, search audit, offer proposals.
- Founder 1: decide Vercel plan (payments on Hobby), approve/reject OFFER proposals P1-P9, confirm the "every message is read by a person" commitment on /feedback; send research form to 10 known developers.
- Founder 2: record V1/V2 from the live product; review POSITIONING and LANDING-VARIANTS; set up Search Console and Bing verification (docs/seo/MEASUREMENT.md).
- Exit check: dashboard shows consenting visits; Free capacity alert understood; 10+ research responses requested.

## Days 15-30: small authorized tests and feedback
- Post Show HN (one founder, one post) and 1-2 clips; publish guides 1-2; keep every claim to DEMO-RUNS.
- Read research responses weekly; log themes with the method in RESEARCH-KIT.md; do not generalize under 10 responses.
- Fix the top two friction points (likely: wait time/progress, missing-model recovery, free-limit message, verifier independence).
- Exit check: >= 500 qualified visits for at least one channel, or a note on why not (then fix distribution, not message).

## Days 31-60: improve the strongest message, retention, content
- Keep the channel with the better visit -> activated rate; stop the other if it is under 0.25% after 500 visits.
- Publish guides 3-5 and clips 3-5; tutorials T1/T2.
- Retention: weekly cohort view; cancel-reason review; fix churn causes before adding acquisition (RETENTION.md).
- Permissioned case studies: only with a user's explicit written permission, quoting what they wrote, never invented; none exist yet.
- Exit check: activated users count; first paying customers? If < 3 payers after 100 activated users, review price/offer fit (OFFER.md) before more traffic.

## Days 61-90: expand only what is supported
- Add the next 2-3 guides on decisions that readers asked about; consider Product Hunt only if gate 1 is passed; consider trial credits (P2) or annual plan (P5) with owner approval; evaluate a paid-acquisition *proposal* only with budget and loss limit written down.
- Exit check: the model's assumptions replaced with measured values (update the yellow cells), and a decision on whether the segment holds.
