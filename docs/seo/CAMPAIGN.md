Rules that apply to every draft
- Keplar is **not** superintelligence and not AGI. Never imply it is. Say it is a practical multi-model workspace: "One question. Multiple intelligences. One answer." The step-toward framing is modest: it combines several models' answers into one.
- No invented numbers, testimonials, ratings, customer counts, speed or accuracy claims. The Consensus figure in the product is how strongly the consulted models agree. It is not an accuracy or confidence score.
- Quote definitions from the primary sources listed in `KEYWORDS.md`, with the access date (3 Oct 2026), and mark timelines as contested opinion.
- Every link carries UTM tags: `?utm_source=<site>&utm_medium=<type>&utm_campaign=si-cluster` (the site stores them in the visitor's browser only when Analytics is allowed).
- Free plan wording: free models only, up to 50 questions a day (fair use).

## Demo scripts (for a screen recording; record a real run, do not stage or edit results)

### Demo 1: "Ask five models what superintelligence means" (90 s, for Show HN / Product Hunt / posts)
1. (0–10 s) Open keplar.one, no signup. Narration: "Keplar sends one question to several AI models and writes one answer. It isn't superintelligence, it's a workspace."
2. (10–30 s) Type: *What is the difference between AGI and superintelligence?* and send.
3. (30–60 s) Show the answer as it forms: open **Models consulted**, then **Disagreements**. Say only what is on screen.
4. (60–80 s) Point at **Consensus**: "This shows how strongly the models agree. It's not a score for being right."
5. (80–90 s) Close on the tagline "One question. Multiple intelligences. One answer." and the link to `/ai-vs-agi-vs-superintelligence`.
Do not state timings, model counts or quality unless they are visible in that run.

### Demo 2: "Where do models disagree about AI timelines?" (60 s, for X/LinkedIn)
1. Ask: *When will superintelligence arrive?* Expect disagreement; show the **Disagreements** section only if it appears.
2. Narration: "Researchers disagree too. In the 2023 AI Impacts survey of 2,778 researchers, the aggregate forecast for machines outperforming humans at every task was 10% by 2027 and 50% by 2047, with a wide spread." Link `/superintelligence#timelines` (check the anchor exists) and cite arXiv:2401.02843.
3. End: "Opinion, not fact. Sources and dates on the page."

### Demo 3: "SI: which meaning?" (45 s, explainer)
1. Screen: the `/si-vs-ai` page; read the three meanings (SI units, superintelligence, the US executive branch's new name for AI per Executive Order 14434).
2. Show the Federal Register entry (91 FR 63129, 2 Oct 2026) next to the page's quote.
3. Close: "If you search 'SI AI', this is the sorted-out version." No Keplar pitch beyond the closing link.
